Chapter 2 — The funnel confirms the signal

Each row shows candidates at their current latest status — these are mutually exclusive snapshots, not a waterfall. A candidate counted at Interview is NOT also counted at Screening. This means stage totals do not need to decrease from left to right — a candidate who is currently at Interview was never counted at Screening in this view. The rising A/B percentage at each stage is the signal: Fit Score is concentrating high-fit candidates at every decision point.

Funnel methodology — important to understand: Stage counts show how many candidates are currently sitting at that status as their latest recorded status in SuccessFactors (Jan–Jun 2026). They do not show cumulative flow. Example: if 100 candidates applied and 60 were screened, the Applied row shows the 40 still at Applied and the Screening row shows the 60 who were screened. The A/B % at each stage = (A+B) ÷ (A+B+C+No Fit) — scored candidates only. Stage names and status mappings confirmed by Jakub on July 15, 2026.

⚠ Open item: Child requisition Fit Score linkage
PMI uses a parent/child requisition structure. The parent requisition has the job ad and matching criteria — this is where Fit Score is generated. Child requisitions are created at offer stage and have blank matching criteria by design, which is why they show as "Incomplete Job" and cannot be scored.

Current treatment: Child requisition hires are counted separately with no Fit Score. We are not linking child hires back to the parent's Fit Score in this version of the dashboard.

Status: A mechanism to match candidate ID across parent and child requisitions to retrieve the parent Fit Score is under investigation with the engineering team. There is no confirmed timeline yet. We will update you as soon as this is resolved.

Impact: The 1,074 "Incomplete Job" hires (19.4% of all hires) are likely majority child req hires. If these could be linked to parent scores, the overall A/B hire rate would improve — the true scored rate may be higher than 72.3%.
Action 1 — Improve job configuration in Commercial Operations and Operations
Commercial Operations: 1,353 of 2,602 hires (52%) have no score — the highest No Score rate of any major category. Operations: 783 of 1,323 hires (59%) have no score. Both are heavily impacted by child requisitions. Short-term action: ensure parent requisitions in these categories have complete matching criteria set before posting. This is the single highest-leverage action available.
Action 2 — Continue Gen 3 rollout to reduce Unsupported Language group
Gen 3 is now active for PMI and covers 17 languages with cross-lingual scoring — meaning a French CV against an English job description will now be scored. The 1,234 "Unsupported Language" hires from H1 2026 will shrink significantly in H2 2026 as Gen 3 processes these cross-lingual pairs. No action required from PMI — this improvement happens automatically.
Dashboard limitations — what belongs in Report Builder instead: Dynamic filtering by Country, Salary Grade, and Function is not supported in this dashboard. The dashboard is an analytical summary view. For row-level recruiter data, individual candidate scores, filtering by date sub-range, or drill-down by specific country or function, the Report Builder export (currently being configured with Leon) is the right tool.
