How to read this funnel

Each stage shows how many unique candidate–job pairs ever passed through that stage at any point during January–June 2026 — regardless of where they ended up. A candidate who applied, was screened, interviewed, and then hired is counted in all four stages. This is a progression funnel, not a snapshot. The numbers decrease as candidates are filtered at each gate, and the A/B share at each stage shows whether Fit Score is concentrating stronger candidates as the process advances.

How this differs from the v9 dashboard Funnel tab: The v9 funnel showed candidates at their current latest status only — mutually exclusive, so a candidate at Interview was not counted at Screening. This view counts everyone who ever passed each stage, as requested on August 31, 2026.
