⚠ Open item: Child requisition Fit Score linkage
PMI uses a parent/child requisition structure. Child requisitions have blank matching criteria by design, which triggers "Incomplete Job" score status. Currently these hires are counted without a Fit Score. A mechanism to match candidate ID across parent and child requisitions is under engineering investigation — no confirmed timeline. The true A/B rate for scored hires is likely higher than 71.2% once child req hires are correctly attributed to parent scores.
Action 1 — Gen 3 rollout reducing Unsupported Language group
Gen 3 is active for PMI since July 1, 2026 and covers 17 languages with cross-lingual scoring. The Unsupported Language no-score group is shrinking automatically. No action required from PMI — this improvement happens automatically and is already visible in the data.
Action 2 — Improve job configuration in Commercial Operations and Operations
Commercial Operations and Operations have the highest No Score rates — strongly suggesting child requisition concentration. Short-term action: ensure parent requisitions in these categories have complete matching criteria set before posting. This is the single highest-leverage action available to improve the overall A/B hire rate.
