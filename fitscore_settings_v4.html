Score recalculation
Decide when a candidate's Fit Score is allowed to change after it has first been assigned.
Dynamic
Scores update continuously. Any change to a candidate's profile or to the job — including job title, description, matching criteria, years of experience, or location — triggers a fresh calculation for all affected candidates.
Lock after first score
A candidate's score for a job is permanently locked once first assigned. No changes — to the candidate's profile or to the job — will trigger a rescore. Use when strict score stability is required for compliance or audit purposes.
Use with care. Candidates will never see their score change — even when the job description materially changes. This may affect match quality over time and is not the Phenom-recommended approach.
Lock after first score, rescore on job changes Recommended
A candidate's score is locked once first assigned and does not change if they update their profile. However, any change to the job — including title, description, matching criteria, years of experience, or location — automatically rescores all candidates for that job, keeping match quality accurate.
